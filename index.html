FAQ

Common questions

What is Shuttle.Direct?

Shuttle.Direct is a free comparison and quote service for airport shuttles, private transfers and group transport. Tell us your trip once and matched local operators send fixed-price quotes.

Is it free to request a quote?

Yes. Requesting quotes is free and there is no obligation to book.

Shared shuttle or private transfer — which is cheaper?

For 1–2 travellers a shared shuttle is usually cheapest. From about 3 passengers a private car or van often costs the same or less per person and is faster.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Include your flight number in the request. Most operators track flights and adjust the pickup time, and many include free waiting time after landing.
