Help centre & FAQ

Everything you need to know about quotes, rides and listings.

Is Shuttle.Direct a transport company?

No. We are a comparison and quote-matching service. Rides are provided by independent licensed operators and our instant-book partners.

How much does it cost to use?

Requesting quotes is free for travellers. Operators may pay for premium listings, and we may earn affiliate commissions from partner bookings.

How quickly will I get a quote?

Most requests receive responses within 24 hours. For travel within 24 hours, use an instant-book partner.

Do prices include tolls and tips?

Operators are asked to quote all-inclusive prices including tolls and taxes. Tipping customs vary by country — see our tipping guide.

What if my flight is delayed?

Always include your flight number. Most operators monitor flights and include free waiting time after landing (commonly 45–90 minutes at airports).

Can I cancel?

Cancellation terms are set by the operator you book with. Many offer free cancellation up to 24–48 hours before pickup — check your quote.

Do you provide child seats?

Yes, on request. Specify the number of infants and children and the seats needed in your quote request.

Are vehicles wheelchair accessible?

Accessible vehicles are available in many cities. Select ‘Accessible’ in the vehicle step and describe your needs.

How do you vet operators?

Operators must confirm an operating licence and commercial passenger insurance before listing. Verified badges are shown for operators whose documents we have reviewed.

I run a shuttle company. How do I join?

Apply on our Operators page. Basic listings are free.

Still need help?

Our team replies within one business day.
