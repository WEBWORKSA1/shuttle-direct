Privacy policy

Your privacy matters. Here’s exactly what we do with your data.

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

Information you submit in forms (such as name, contact details and trip details), and basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited.

How we use it

  • To prepare and send transfer quotes, including sharing trip details with matched transport operators.
  • To reply to inquiries, applications, pledges and contest entries.
  • To send newsletters if you opt in (unsubscribe anytime).

Advertising & cookies

We may use Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. Embedded YouTube videos load in privacy-enhanced mode only after you click play.

Affiliate links

Some outbound links are affiliate links; partners may set cookies to attribute bookings.

Form processing

Form submissions are delivered by a third-party form-processing service. We keep submissions only as long as needed to handle your request.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data via our contact page.
