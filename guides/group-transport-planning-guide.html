Great event transport is invisible — guests simply arrive on time. Here is how professional planners build a transport plan.

1. Build the manifest

List guests, arrival times, flights and hotels. Cluster arrivals into windows (e.g. every 90 minutes) instead of booking per person.

2. Choose the vehicle mix

  • Under 15 guests: vans or minibuses.
  • 15–60 guests: coaches for peak moves, vans for stragglers.
  • VIPs and couples: sedans or luxury cars.

3. Loops vs. dedicated runs

For hotel–venue shuttles, run continuous loops at fixed intervals (e.g. every 20 minutes). Calculate: round-trip time ÷ interval = vehicles needed.

Example: a 40-minute round trip with a 20-minute interval needs 2 vehicles running the loop.

4. Budget realistically

Coaches are usually priced by the hour with a minimum (often 4–5 hours). Late-night returns, driver overtime and multi-day hire change prices — include them in the brief.

5. Send a complete brief

Dates, guest count, pickup and drop-off points, timings, accessibility needs and budget. The more complete the brief, the more accurate the quotes.
