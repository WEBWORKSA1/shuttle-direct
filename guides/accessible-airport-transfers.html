Accessible ground transport is widely available in major cities, but the details matter. Ask these questions before confirming.

Key questions

  1. Is the vehicle ramp- or lift-equipped, and what is the maximum wheelchair weight?
  2. Can the passenger remain seated in the wheelchair, and are tie-downs provided?
  3. What are the door height and interior headroom?
  4. Is there room for a companion and luggage?
  5. Is the driver trained in securing mobility devices?

Tell the airline too

Request airport assistance with your airline at least 48 hours in advance so staff can bring you to the meeting point where your driver is waiting.

Booking on Shuttle.Direct

Select “Accessible” as your vehicle and describe the chair (manual or powered, folding or not) in the notes field. Matched operators will confirm their equipment in the quote.
