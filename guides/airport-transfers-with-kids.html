Travelling with kids changes the transfer equation. The cheapest option on paper rarely works with a car seat, stroller, three suitcases and a tired toddler.

Car seats: request them in advance

Most private transfer operators can supply infant seats (rear-facing), convertible seats and boosters — but only if you request them. Give each child’s age and approximate weight in your quote request.

Choosing the vehicle

  • Family of 3–4 with standard luggage: SUV or MPV.
  • Family of 5–7, or with strollers and oversize bags: minivan.
  • Two families travelling together: an 8-seat van usually costs less than two cars.

Arrival tips

  • Choose meet & greet so the driver helps with bags while you manage the kids.
  • Keep snacks, wipes and a charged tablet in hand luggage for the ride.
  • For long transfers to resorts, ask about a mid-way stop.
Shared shuttles rarely guarantee child seats. For under-4s, a private transfer is strongly recommended.
