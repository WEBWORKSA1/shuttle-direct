Most transfer problems come from missing details. Use this checklist every time you request a quote.

  1. Exact pickup point — airport and terminal, or full hotel name and address.
  2. Flight number — lets the operator track delays automatically.
  3. Landing time, not departure time — for arrivals, give the scheduled arrival.
  4. Passenger count by age — adults, children and infants determine seat requirements.
  5. Luggage — count suitcases and flag oversize items like skis, golf bags or strollers.
  6. Child seats — specify infant, convertible or booster.
  7. Accessibility needs — wheelchair type and whether it folds.
  8. Return trip — booking both ways together often earns a better rate.
  9. Contact method abroad — WhatsApp is the easiest for most drivers.
  10. Waiting-time policy — confirm how long the driver waits free after landing.
Tip: Screenshot your confirmation with the driver’s phone number before you fly — airport Wi-Fi can be unreliable.

How far in advance?

For individual transfers, 2–7 days ahead is plenty in most cities. For holidays, big events and groups, book 3–8 weeks ahead.
