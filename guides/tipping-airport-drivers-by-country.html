Tipping customs vary enormously. Here is a practical guide for the most visited destinations. Always check whether a gratuity is already included in your quote.

North America

  • USA: 15–20% for taxis and private drivers; $2–$5 per bag for shared shuttle drivers who handle luggage.
  • Canada: 10–15% is standard.
  • Mexico: 10% or a few dollars; tips in pesos are appreciated.

Europe

  • UK: round up or 10% for good service.
  • France, Spain, Italy: rounding up is common; 5–10% for exceptional help.
  • Germany, Netherlands: round up to the next euro or add around 5–10%.

Middle East & Asia

  • UAE, Qatar: small tips are appreciated but not expected.
  • India, Sri Lanka: a modest tip for help with luggage is customary.
  • Japan: tipping is not customary and can cause confusion.
  • Singapore, Hong Kong: not expected; rounding up is fine.
  • Thailand: round up or add a small amount.

Oceania & Africa

  • Australia, New Zealand: not expected; rounding up for great service.
  • South Africa: around 10% is customary.
Pre-booked private transfers in many countries already include service — check the quote before adding more.
